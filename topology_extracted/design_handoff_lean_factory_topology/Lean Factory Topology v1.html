Design notes

U-shape rationale. Material enters at the NW dock and exits at the SW dispatch — short operator walk, full-cell visibility from the plant office in the U's interior.

Chaotic start state. Ops are under-staffed at complex nodes (FA-03/04 doubled, Final QA doubled). CTs are deliberately unbalanced — the first problems the player will see are flow pile-ups at Integration 3.

v1 support surfaces. Maintenance crib and Plant Office sit inside the U so the player can pan there without leaving the cell.
