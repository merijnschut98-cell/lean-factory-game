# UX audit, 2026-10-27: ranked findings before Merijn's Friday playtest

**What this is.** This audit looks at the real game's screens through the eyes of a first-time Dutch player, judged against the immersion directive (CLAUDE.md §2), phase 2 design §1.8 (the phone) and §2 (talks), the Dutch style guide (§4 "Never the game") and the first-five-minutes spec (§1, §3). It lists findings only. No game code was changed.

**How the screenshots were made.** They come from `game/tools/play_shot.gd` and `game/tools/creator_screen_shot.gd` under `xvfb-run`, one Godot process at a time, rendered at 1920×1080 on branch `claude/compassionate-heisenberg-ynlqyr` (`8e1d1da`). 84 states were taken, from the title through the opening, office at 08:30, 12:30, 18:30 and 20:00 (`--at`), the phone apps, both talk modes, the A3, the Check, the board, the codex, the KPI board, the van, the yard, toasts and the terugblik. 28 are kept under `docs/screenshots/ux-audit/` at 1280×720 with a 128-colour palette, so gradients may band. The creator shot keeps full colour. The project stretches a 1600×900 canvas (`canvas_items`), so a UI font of N px shows as 1.2·N px at 1080p. `UiTheme.SMALL` (17) reads as 20 px, the talk summary's 15 as 18 px, the minimap's 12 as 14 px and the talk's key numbers (11) as 13 px.

**Not counted as findings (tool artefacts).** The shot tools render without a portrait photo, so every badge shows the blue silhouette. The `talk_phone` shot is staged at 08:30.

**Size:** S is under half a day, M is one to two days, L is more. **Who:** *build now* means it can be built without a design decision; *needs Merijn* means a decision of his comes first.
