# Playtest checklist: Act 0 and Act I (Merijn)

Build: the **Playtest** release on GitHub (the repo's Releases → Playtest → `Spanning-windows-playtest.zip`; unzip and run `Spanning.exe`). It is rebuilt on every commit whose subject starts with `[playtest]`.
Aim: play as a new CI lead would, not as the designer. Note what you *feel*, not only what is broken.
Time: about 60–90 minutes for Act 0 plus the start of Act I. Stop whenever you like; that is data too.

## Before you start
- [ ] Unzip and start the game. The title menu should open, not the office.
- [ ] Have a notepad (or phone) ready. Write a line the moment something confuses, bores or pleases you, with the in-game day and time.
- [ ] Optional, best value: let one or two friends play the first five minutes **cold** before you (they have never seen the game; you watch and say nothing).

## 1. The first five minutes (cold test)
- [ ] "Nieuw spel" → HR's mail → the phone camera. Make your character. Did any option feel missing or wrong for an 18–35 Dutch player?
- [ ] Reception: Yvonne and the badge with your photo. Did it feel like *you* arriving?
- [ ] Ruud's walk: does he lead clearly? Do you remember who you met?
- [ ] The whiteboard explainer (spanning, stroom, weerstand): could you say the one idea back in your own words afterwards?
- [ ] Scenes: lines wait for your key (Space/Enter/click; first press completes, second goes on), ← re-reads, holding Space skips to the next point where you act, Esc pauses everything. Natural?
- [ ] The El Amrani letter, the evening (the office dark, one lamp at Anouk's desk), Anouk at seven.
- [ ] The Dutch: does anyone sound translated or written? Note the line (and compare with `docs/qa/dutch-sample-2026-10-19.md`).
- [ ] **Acceptance:** afterwards, answer (or ask your friend) without looking:
  - *Wat kom je hier doen?*
  - *Wie heb je ontmoet?* (at least three of Ruud, Sanne, Joost, Marco, Anouk)
- [ ] Rough time from "Nieuw spel" to the first talk (target ≤ 6 minutes). Too long anywhere? Where?

## 2. The place menu (your earlier complaint)
At **Intake** and **Werkvoorbereiding**, each day of the training week:
- [ ] Only a few options, and each one makes sense *at that place*.
- [ ] You can talk to or ask someone at both places on Monday.
- [ ] Every action gives back something real (an answer, an observation), never "niets nieuws".
- [ ] The right option does **not** stand out because the others are obviously wrong.
- [ ] New tools arrive through the week (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday) and Ruud's message on your phone explains them. Did getting a new tool feel like a reward?
- [ ] Still repetitive or boring anywhere? Where?

## 3. The talks (your feedback here decides the Act I rewrite)
- [ ] While they speak the camera is on their face; when you choose it pulls back to the two of you, with four sentences. Does it feel like two people talking, or still like a menu?
- [ ] Their body shows how open they are (arms folded, turned away, nodding, leaning in), and only the small trust pips and the ▲/▼ after your line are on screen. Can you tell how it is going without the old bars?
- [ ] The four sentences: does one obviously stand out as "the right one"? Does any sound like something you would never say?
- [ ] Afterwards: the summary in Notities (phone), Ruud naming the move in week one. Helpful or patronising?
- [ ] A mission now waits until you have brought its owner along in a talk. Fair, or a wall? (Pressure works too, at half pay.)
- [ ] Try one talk badly on purpose (pressure early). Did it cost something fair, and could you retry the next day?
- [ ] A phone call (El Amrani or Ilse from the KCC): does it work without a face?

## 4. The week and Act I
- [ ] Thursday's experiment and Friday's result: did you understand why it worked or didn't?
- [ ] The first Check: did the numbers tell a story you could follow?
- [ ] Act I: pick up a main mission. Find the first key clue. Roughly how long did it take? (target ≤ 5 real minutes)
- [ ] Did anything feel like being *handed* the answer?
- [ ] Did you ever not know what to do next? For how long, and where?

## 5. World and immersion
- [ ] The phone (Tab; J/M/K open Notities, Kaart, Begrippen): Berichten, Opdrachten, Notities, Kaart, Wie is wie, Instellingen. Does it feel like *your* phone, and do you miss anything that used to be on screen?
- [ ] The objective line top left plus the minimap marker: did you always know your next step?
- [ ] The office keeps time: people come in by the lift, take coffee around ten, walk to the kantine at lunch, go home after five; the light changes. Does it feel inhabited?
- [ ] People turn to you and greet you by name once a day; E on someone with nothing to discuss gives a quick line; two at the coffee corner chat. Too much, too little, right?
- [ ] The stacks at Intake and the in-tray at Werkvoorbereiding grow and shrink with the work. Did you notice? Did it make you curious?
- [ ] Sound: the office murmur, footsteps, the lift, doors, the phone buzz. Listen especially to the door, the voices and the vans (picked without being heard). Settings: "Geluid uit" and the volume.
- [ ] After five: walk to the front door on the begane grond, "Naar huis", the next morning. Does ending a day feel right?
- [ ] Walk the floors and the yard (through the kantine's back door).
- [ ] Note every moment that broke the spell: a menu over the world, a number where a person could have shown it, generic text where your name could have stood.

## 6. Saving and stopping
- [ ] Save into a slot from the pause menu (Esc), go to the title menu, and continue from it.
- [ ] Quit mid-day and restart: does the game pick up where you were, with a short recap from Ruud?

## Afterwards (5 minutes)
- [ ] Three things that pulled you in.
- [ ] Three things that pushed you out.
- [ ] The one moment you almost stopped playing, and why.
- [ ] Would you play tomorrow? Why, or why not?
- [ ] Share the playtest log with me: `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Spanning\saves\playtest_log.txt` (local only, it records game events and times, never your name).

Bring your notes to the next session. Your answers decide what comes first, together with the two open design questions in PLAN 2.12 (neglect pays slightly; ignoring people costs nothing).
