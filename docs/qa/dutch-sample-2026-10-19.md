## 5. bank.player proposal

The shared fallback sentences, one list per move, for the coordinator to apply once agent B's list format has landed (B owns `bank.player`). Each variant fits any person; the move is never named. Experience stays inside this job (the player's past is the player's own, so no "bij mijn vorige werk"). The first variant of each list is the current line or its natural fix.

```yaml
  player:
    mood:
      - "Hoe gaat het met je? Echt, bedoel ik."
      - "Hoe is het vandaag?"
      - "Alles goed met je?"
      - "Hoe zit je erbij, deze week?"
    open_question:
      - "Hoe ziet jouw week eruit?"
      - "Waar ben je nu mee bezig?"
      - "Vertel eens, hoe gaat dat hier meestal?"

      - "Ik krijg het idee dat je je er zorgen over maakt."
    empathy:
      - "Dat is ook niet niks, als je zo je best doet."
```

## 6. What I would like your ear on

- **"Je treft het niet"** (Joost, #9): natural to me, but some regions say "Je hebt pech". Fine for a man from Utrecht?
- **Ruud's wife joke** (#4): replaces "De lift is voor de koffie", which nobody understood. His sheet allows self-mocking about his age; a wife's remark may feel too personal on day one.
- **"diegene"** (#14): correct but a bit written; "vraag eerst hoe het met ze gaat" is more spoken, but singular "ze" for one person may read as plural.
- **"Staan we er weer voor niks"** (#7): monteurs' talk for a wasted trip; check it reads clearly to someone outside the trade.
- **"Daar word je ook gek van"** (#34): the player says it, so it must not sound like they judge her; alternatives "Dat moet frustrerend zijn" (more written) or "Dat lijkt me slopend".
- **Fenna** (#22): Anouk's child was Noor, the same name as Noor Hendriks (PO, Act IV) and a common player name in tests; renamed. Say if you want another name.
- **Explainer, r3** (#19): "komt via een andere draad weer terug" is more correct than before (the return is the neutral), but adds a word the analogy did not have. Simpler: "en komt weer terug".
