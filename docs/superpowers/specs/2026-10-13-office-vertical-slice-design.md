# The connections office: first 3D vertical slice — design

> Status: Merijn chose the 3D world first, a semi-realistic style, and one building with one mission. He also asked me to design and then build (2026-10-13). The design calls below are Claude's and are listed in PLAN.md as pending Merijn.

## Goal
The first time the game is played rather than tested. The player walks through Voltana's connections office in Zuidmark and does the gemba work with the existing rules: investigate, write the A3, run the experiment, Check and Act. They solve one mission end to end: **"Maatwerk voor standaardwerk"** (`fix_custom_design`, problem `custom_design` at `connections.design`).

## The office (what you see)
- **One floor of a 1990s Dutch office building, refurbished:** carpet tiles, white plaster walls, a light wood-look and grey desk landscape, tall windows with daylight, suspended ceiling with light panels, Voltana-blue accents.
- **Intake corner (`connections.intake`):** 2 desks with monitors, a tray of incoming applications and a phone.
- **Werkvoorbereiding (`connections.design`):** 4 desks with dual monitors, rolled-up drawings, a plotter and a big stack of dossiers (the backlog, visible WIP).
- **Obeya wall:** a whiteboard with a KPI board and sticky notes, where the player writes the A3 and talks to the coach.
- **Coach's room:** a glass-walled office for Ingrid, the senior manager (the coach).
- **Mission board** by the entrance, and a coffee corner for flavour.
- **No people yet:** the desks show their work (screens, papers, coats on chairs), and the team "answers" through the UI. Characters come later; whether an AI model generator is used for them is Merijn's call.

## How it is made
- **Blender, scripted** (`tools/blender/build_office.py`, `bpy` from PyPI), exported as `game/assets/office/office.glb`.
- **Textures** are generated by script (`tools/blender/textures.py`, numpy and PIL): wood, carpet tiles, plaster, concrete and painted metal, each with a normal map. glTF only carries image textures, so nothing is procedural at runtime. The files are named so that CC0 photo textures (Poly Haven, ambientCG) can replace them later.
- **Lighting:** the Compatibility renderer (needed for the web export) has no realtime global illumination. Blender bakes ambient occlusion and light into a lightmap texture on the room shell; Godot adds a sun through the windows, soft shadows and the existing environment.
- **Interaction points** are empties in the glb named `place:connections.intake`, `place:connections.design`, `obeya`, `coach` and `mission_board`. The Godot scene reads them, so art and logic stay apart.

## How it plays
- **First person**, WASD and the mouse; E to interact. Looking at a place shows a Dutch prompt, e.g. "E — Werkvoorbereiding (connections · design)".
- **At a team's place:** a menu of gemba tools, each with its time cost (from `Investigation.TOOLS`), and the questions from `Investigation.QUESTIONS`. Using a tool advances the clock and shows the clues found, which go into a clue journal.
- **Obeya wall:** the A3 form.
  - Waste: the 8 wastes.
  - Cause: the options from `Investigation.cause_options`.
  - Evidence: your found clues.
  - Countermeasure: `Region.available_countermeasures`.
  - Prediction: a KPI.
  - Submitting it starts the experiment. "Wacht tot de Check" (wait for the Check) advances the clock.
  - Check and Act panels follow, in Dutch.
- **Coach:** `Missions.ask_coach()`, Kata questions about the main mission only.
- **Mission board:** accept missions. The slice starts with `walk_connections` done (the stream was walked in the story), so `fix_custom_design` is available.
- **HUD:** day and time, budget, XP and belt, the active mission.

## Code
- **`game/src/game/session.gd` (`GameSession`):** owns `Catalog`, `Region` (with `use_kpis`) and `Missions`. One method per player action:
  - `use_tool(place, tool)` advances the clock and returns the clues;
  - `ask`, `submit_a3`, `wait_for_check`, `check`, `act`, `accept`, `ask_coach`.
  Each calls `Missions.update()`. This is pure logic and headless-testable.
- **`game/src/world/office/`:** `office.tscn`, `player.gd` (a CharacterBody3D first-person controller), `interactable.gd`, and `office.gd`, which builds the interaction areas from the glb empties and routes them to the UI.
- **`game/src/ui/`:** HUD, prompt, tool menu, journal, A3 form, coach and result panels, all Control scenes built in code, with Dutch text.
- The main scene becomes the office, and the boot scene stays as the title.

## Tests
- `GameSession`: accepting the mission, then gemba tools finding the key clues, then the A3, wait, Check passed and Act. The mission completes, XP is granted and the clock has advanced.
- Office scene, headless: every interaction empty is found and maps to a valid place in the network.
- Screenshots via `game/tools/screenshot.gd`, attached to the briefing.

## Out of scope (next pieces)
People and characters, other buildings, the town, sound, saving from the UI, and the KPI board UI.
