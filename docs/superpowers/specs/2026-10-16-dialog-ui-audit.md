# Dialog UI audit (PLAN 1.14)

> By the mission-and-dialog design agent, 2026-10-16. Docs only. Audited: the eight screenshots `docs/screenshots/play_dialog_*.png` from the UI agent's worktree (`worktree-agent-a4bf66ca2babbf0b4`, state of 2026-10-16), against the dialog spec (`2026-10-15-dialog-system-design.md` §3.7 the meter, §11 the UI sketch), Merijn's decisions (PLAN §9: a bar that fills, ▲/▼ arrows, never a number; all moves always visible; the energy bar) and CLAUDE.md §2 (immersion; nothing handed over; respect the player's time; dialogs are give and take).
>
> **For the UI agent.** §1 is the note: concrete changes in priority order, each with the screenshot it refers to. §2 is what already works and must stay. §3 lists two spec fixes made with this audit.

## Verdict

The dialog UI is **sound in its rules and readable as text**: the meter is a bar that fills, the arrows carry no numbers, the band name is always written, all fifteen moves are visible, the proposals in the picker look alike, and nothing marks a reply as right. The weak spots are **immersion** (you talk to a flat icon over a room you cannot see the person in, with the budget still on screen), **two styling choices that judge moves** (grey pressure chips, a green selected chip), **a summary card that does not say what comes next**, and **one overlap bug**. None of it needs a rule change.

## 2. What works and must stay

- **The meter follows Merijn's decision:** a bar that fills, band colour plus the band word ("Op z'n hoede", "Open", "Denkt mee"), ▲▲ / ▼▼ beside the bar after a reply, **no number anywhere** (mid, ruined). Nothing shows before the first move (start).
- **Nothing is handed over:** all eight listening and seven steering moves are always there; no chip is highlighted for fit (apart from U4/U5); the four KPI pairs in the picker look alike (picks); the hidden proposal is not shown before it is earned.
- **The layer tracker** (● Standpunt ● Belang ○ Zorg) is small, honest and readable, and the summary's "Wat je hoorde" quotes each layer in the person's words (summary) — the strongest thing on the card.
- **The player's last line in grey above the reply** ("Jij: Je zit klem tussen …") makes the exchange readable as a conversation (mid).
- **A ruin is a setback, not an end:** "Mislukt — morgen weer", the trace going down, and "Bespreek met Ruud Hoekstra" right there (ruined).
- **"Een nieuwe aanwijzing staat in je journaal (J)."** in green on the summary: the concern became evidence (summary).
- **The talk time is honest:** the clock moves when the talk ends (08:30 during, 08:54 after), not mid-sentence (spec §3.5).

## 3. Quit risks, in short

1. **A ruin on the first try with nothing to hold on to** (ruined): many players will push first. U6 (the retry named) and U7 (where it went down) turn it into a lesson; Ruud's after-ruin question is already one click away.
2. **The panel covers the world** (start → summary, up to 70 % of the screen): the player stops feeling they are in Voltana (U2, U8, U9).
3. **A grey chip that is not disabled** (U4): a player who clicks it and sees it work feels tricked; one who never clicks it never learns what pressure does.

## 4. Spec fixes made with this audit

- Dialog spec §3.7 and §11 still said the ▲/▼ flashes "with the number"; Merijn decided arrows without numbers (PLAN §9, 2026-10-15). Both lines now say ▲/▲▲ or ▼/▼▼ by size of the change, never a number.
