# The yard (de werf): build spec (PLAN 2.2)

> Status: world agent (Claude, 2026-10-17), for the world agent's own build. Merijn was away; the calls are logged in PLAN §10 under the world agent. Read with PLAN §5.1 (seams, not loading screens; gemba readability), §5.2 (the campus), `content/world.yaml`, `content/missions.yaml`, `content/problems_tier1.yaml`.

## 1. Why now

The field half of tier 1 has nowhere to stand. `content/world.yaml` puts no room around `outages.*`, `meters.visit`, `meters.reserve_meter`, `connections.materials`, `connections.execution` or `connections.commissioning`, so the missions that lead there (below) have no place to visit in the 3D office. Until the town exists (Phase 4, 4.1) the yard is where the monteurs' work is seen: the vans they take, the stock they take with them, the room where the storingsdienst waits and the bench where Henk shows how a joint is made.

## 2. Where it joins the head office (the seam)

- **The back door of the kantine** on the begane grond, in the west gable wall (the long walls are ribbon windows): a door with a green exit sign and "Werf". E (or a click on its ring) takes you out; on the yard the same door, from outside, takes you back in. It works like the stairs: the floor's model is swapped, the player stands at the other side of the door, nothing else changes (no loading screen, no time passes).
- **The yard lies west of the building, in the building's own coordinates** (Blender x -52..-18), so the building's west facade stands at the yard's east edge and the door is the same door from both sides.
- In the building data it is an **outside area of the begane grond**: `floors` gets `{id: yard, name: Werf, outside_of: ground, model: office_yard.glb}`. It has no level of its own (the stairs never lead there, the lift says "De werf bereik je via de achterdeur in de kantine."); the M map shows it with the floors, as "Werf".
- Markers: `exit:yard` and `arrive:exit` inside the kantine; `exit:ground` and `arrive:exit` on the yard.

## 4. What is visible (gemba tells, ambiguous)

Each tell can be normal; only the tools and the questions settle it (§5.1, "nothing is handed over").
- **The uitgiftelijst** on Dirk's counter (review of 3c40ed8..381d21c #3; it replaced a van kanban wall, which was wrong_joint_in_van's best countermeasure `two_bin_van_kanban` standing in the yard from day one): a clipboard where the monteurs sign out what they take, a line each (date, bus, what, paraaf). A long list may be a busy week of outages, or the same bus back for material the same day.
- **An empty rack bay**: the joint shelf (moffen) has one bay empty among full ones. Stock out, or stock moved?
- **A van with its back doors open** and its shelves a jumble of bins, next to a van whose shelves are labelled. Which one carries the wrong joint cannot be seen from the camera.
- **The on-call board** in the werkplaats with the night's callouts written on it; the cable drums in a row.
None of these change colour or blink; none is a marker; the minimap draws none of them.

## 5. People and the day

- **Dirk Smits** stands at the magazijn counter (always, during the working day: the magazijn is his).
- **Henk and Ahmed** move from the 1e huddle table to the werkplaats for their missions (they are monteurs; the werkplaats is their room).
- **The vans leave in the morning and come back in the afternoon** (monteurs leaving and returning): before 09:00 and after 16:15 (moved from 07:45 on 2026-10-24 so the player sees the morning) every bay holds a van; in between, only the two storingsbussen (on call) stay. Read from `GameSession.minute_of_day`, as lunch is. Walking monteurs (a departure scene) wait for 1.3c's clips.

## 6. Build

- `tools/blender/build_yard.py` (sibling of `build_office.py`, reusing its materials, kits, `MeshBuilder`, collision and markers): the yard's paving and bays, the magazijn and werkplaats as single-storey sheds (walls, a roof the camera cuts away, doors as gaps), racks, counter, workbench, drums, vans (boxes with a cab, Voltana livery from the signage atlas), the uitgiftelijst, a fence, the head office's west facade with the back door. No lightmaps (outdoors, Godot's sun and shadows); `shell_floor` names the ground so the camera and the map find it.
- The begane grond gets the back door and its two markers (`build_office.py`, `--no-bake`).
- Godot: `OfficeBuilding` knows outside areas (`outside_of`; `next_floor` skips them); `OfficeFloors` uses an `exit` like a door seam (prompt "E — Naar de werf" / "E — Naar binnen"); the vans' presence by the hour (`OfficeYard`).
